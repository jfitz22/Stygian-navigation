# 6. Roster, signatures, pacing, threats and repair

## 6.1 Signature definitions [PROPOSED]

Each signature has a **primary proof** (an artefact an expert interprets) and **secondary hints** from other instruments. Hints raise suspicion; proofs settle it.

| Signature | Primary proof | Secondary hints |
|---|---|---|
| **H: Hollow interior** | Lens cavity tomograph (Expert B reads chambers, walls, scale) | Echo Strip double-hump and ringing tail (Expert B); Hearkener "ringing" for very large single voids |
| **M: Worked metal** | Lens lodestone rosette (Expert A reads petal count and symmetry: worked metal is symmetric, ore is ragged) | Camera ember filter shows rust bloom if metal is near the surface; Echo Strip "notch" means a sharp interior edge (stone *or* metal) |
| **C: Geryonite choir** | Decoded triad from the Choir Receiver or Lens C channel (Expert A decodes, needs the medium from Expert B) | Faint triad audible from far away; the number of stacked waves on the scope (1, 2 or 3) |

**Tone families on the Choir Receiver** (all original):
- **Glass** (pure, bell-like): celestial.
- **Buzz** (reedy, sawtooth): fiendish.
- **Bull** (low groan, slow vibrato): Geryon's own.
- **Rime** (cold hiss, pitch-falling): Levistus.

The full **Geryonite triad** is Glass + Buzz + Bull in Elgarz's specific pattern. Other bodies carry one or two families.

### Optional identity clues (no instrument proof needed)

- **The 1,000-mile rule.** The Shed Horn and the Penitent Choir both pass within 1,000 mi of the Tomb at some point during a 30-minute session; Elgarz never does. Players can eliminate them with the DIVIDERS tool and a few fixes.
- **Residual drift.** Elgarz and the Mirrorberg both light DEEP?. Combined with any one signature, this narrows things sharply.
- **Brand repelled [needs your approval].** Coldsteel's old wards reject a brand: "BRAND REPELLED" appears on the chart. Honest and dramatic, though it could be used as a 4-shot identity test. See decisions in section 10.

## 6.4 Remorhazes (local, embodied, recoverable)

**State per remorhaz:** position, `dormant | roused | hunting | at-post | retreating`, target post.

- Three remorhazes live in burrows on the ice shelves around the four posts (the Rookery is the honest source).
- A remorhaz is **roused** when any post's heat bloom (radius `40 + 3h` mi) covers it. It **hunts** the hottest post whose bloom covers it, moving at **1.2 mi/s** (typical travel 45 to 90 s).
- **Indirect visibility:** the post's GROUND needle (a small seismograph next to its thermometer) twitches with approach and rises steadily; camera tilt-down shows steam vents opening in the ice; the Hearkener (if a buoy hydrophone is nearby) shows a short, bright tremor line.
- **On arrival:** if the post's heat is ≥ 40, the post is **damaged**: camera dark, drum jammed, post tab shows a cracked lamp. If heat < 40, it circles for 20 s and burrows (dormant again). The operator can save a post by switching away or venting it in time.
- **Bell rope:** a tiny undocumented pause (5 s) for remorhazes near posts.
- **Repair:** after the remorhaz leaves (30 s after damage, since a damaged post is cold), go to the Engineering Wall, solve the **Linesman's Board** for that post (section 7, Module 4), then pull the sled-dispatch handle. The sled takes 30 to 45 s to reach the post. Total ≈ 60 s. During repair, other posts still work.

## 6.5 The Grindmaw (Styx leviathan)

**State:** position, `roaming | drawn | striking | circling`, target buoy.

- Every ping adds **1.0 noise** to the pinging buoy; noise decays 1% per second (half-life ≈ 70 s).
- When any buoy's noise ≥ 2.0 (two pings within about a minute), the Grindmaw becomes **drawn** to the noisiest buoy and swims at **2.0 mi/s**. It begins roaming ~350 mi from the observatory's buoys, so it typically needs 90 to 180 s; the first warning always appears ≥ 45 s before contact.
- **Indirect visibility:** a broad, low red band walking across bearings on the Hearkener; the Tide Loom arrow at the target buoy shivers; a ping catches it as a huge contact (but adds noise).
- **Diversion:** pinging a different buoy until its noise is higher re-targets it. Players will discover this.
- **Save the buoy:** reeling in takes 20 s (+15 s if the deep line is lowered). A reeled buoy is safe; the Grindmaw circles for 30 s, then roams.
- **Loss:** the buoy is destroyed; its Loom samples remain as aged readings; Sounder unavailable at that position.
- **Redeploy:** Buoy Rack holds 4. The forge-imp builds one every 90 s up to 4 (the rack can never stay empty forever). Reload crank (8 s) + aim on chart + flight (10 s) + settle (20 s) ≈ 40 to 60 s.

## 6.6 Brands (beacon harpoons) and retaliation

- **Stock:** 4 brands; the forge-imp rebuilds one every 4 minutes.
- **Firing:** requires a pinned target and either Lens alignment ≥ 0.5 on it or a fix younger than 30 s. Flight time ≈ distance / 120 mi per second. A miss (the berg moved) plops into the sea and shows as a miss on the chart: honest feedback about tracking quality.
- **Effect:** a gold diamond on the chart that follows the true position forever (until compromised).
- **Retaliation (Leviathan's Cradle):** branding it releases a **brood swarm** that travels toward the nearest buoy or post at 1.5 mi/s and severs it on arrival (same repair flows as above). Foreshadowing: echo traces with "soft walls, pulse" (Expert B's manual warns: *Do not brand what has a heartbeat*), breathing plumes on camera, heartbeat on the Hearkener. In retrospect, every clue was there.
- **Compromise:** a branded berg that breaks apart in a storm (one authored event) loses its brand. Optional.

## 6.7 The payoff [PROPOSED, needs your approval]

1. **Bound lock** at the Moorage Lectern. The Lens now holds Elgarz perfectly at any time.
2. **Full deep scan** of Elgarz (if not already complete) prints the **Coldsteel Tomograph**: a stylised cross-section of the citadel inside the glacier. This doubles as a real GM handout for the next dungeon (a deliberately partial map: major halls, the chain shaft, the buried gate).
3. **Anchorage Litany** (rune keypad, Module 1 in its second role) wakes **the Bull's Mooring**: a Geryonite anchorage chain on Elgarz's southern face draws up a landing stage of black ice. Tabletop effect (suggestions for you to pick from):
   - The party's ship can dock at the Mooring Gate, bypassing the glacier's outer defences.
   - A **Lodestar Shard** pops out of the Lectern: it points toward Elgarz for 7 days.
   - The pneumatic tube delivers the last message of the observatory's sentry captain, with a hint about Coldsteel's current master.
4. **Partial outcomes are still useful.** Strong lock without the anchorage still tells them exactly where Elgarz is. No outcome cancels the session.

## 6.8 Recovery safeguards (no softlock)

| Risk | Safeguard |
|---|---|
| All buoys lost | Forge-imp rebuilds every 90 s; GM "Resupply" button |
| All posts damaged | Repair always possible after remorhaz leaves; remorhazes never attack a cold post |
| Brands exhausted | Rebuild every 4 min; brands are never required |
| Wrong rune entries | Local 15 s cooldown; never permanent |
| Lens never calibrated | Channels still fill at 40% speed |
| Players never lower a deep line | Two-fix tracking plus manual trim still completes a scan (slower) |
| Players miss Elgarz completely | Choir hum strengthens; GM can send a tube canister ("The old Bull sings in the north-east tonight") |
| Furnace overdraw | Breakers trip; nothing breaks |
| Everything goes wrong | GM window: repair all, teleport threat away, advance or rewind the world, grant a signature, force Bound |
