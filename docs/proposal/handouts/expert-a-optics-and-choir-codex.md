# Mockup: Expert A handout, *The Optics & Choir Codex*

**Format proposal:** A5 booklet or US half-letter, 10 to 12 pages, printed double-sided and saddle-stapled; or A4/Letter single-sided for home printing. Warm off-white paper tone, iron-gall brown ink, red rubrication for warnings, large glyph plates. One module per spread. Each page opens with an **"Ask the operator for:"** box.

Below are three sample pages (text and layout only; final art would replace the ASCII glyphs with engraved plates).
