Your mission

Somewhere in this sea drifts Elgarz, the glacier that swallowed Citadel Coldsteel. Find it, prove it, and hit it with a beacon. When a beacon strikes Elgarz, the watch is done.

The machine in one minute

  1. Light the furnace (bottom left). Nothing works until it burns.
  2. The furnace can run only three systems at once: Cameras, Sonar, Radio, Scanner, Currents. Switch them on and off as you need them.
  3. Find ice with the cameras or the sonar. Click a sonar contact or an iceberg on camera to lock onto it.
  4. Test the locked iceberg with the echo printout, radio and scanner.
  5. Fire a beacon to tag it with a colour. Tagged ice stays marked on the chart forever.

Sonar, buoys and the Grindmaw

ASK THE OPERATOR FOR: where the buoy is · how many contacts came back · the ping time
  1. Sonar needs a buoy in the water. Press DROP BUOY, then click inside the dashed launcher line on the chart.
  2. Press PING. The echo takes 6 seconds to come back.
  3. Contacts appear on the sonar and on the chart as green dots. They fade after about a minute.
A contact shows where the ice WAS when you pinged, not where it is now. The ice keeps drifting. Lock onto fresh contacts.

The Grindmaw

A colossal Styx shark, marked in red on the chart at all times. It hunts noise.

  • Three pings in the same area within about two minutes and it comes for the buoy.
  • When it is hunting, the chart shows a dashed red line to your buoy.
  • If it reaches the buoy, the buoy is lost. A new one takes 30 seconds to build.
  • Save the buoy: drop it somewhere else before the Grindmaw arrives. A new area is quiet.

Reading the echo printout

ASK THE OPERATOR FOR: how many bumps after the first tall spike · what the line does at the end (wavy and fading, scratchy, or flat)

Every printout starts with one tall spike. That is the surface of the ice. Ignore it. Count the rounded bumps after it, then look at the tail.

No bumps: SOLID ice. Not hollow.

Bumps with a wavy tail that fades away (it rings): HOLLOW. Each bump is a chamber inside. More bumps, more rooms.

Bumps with a scratchy, ragged tail: SOFT WALLS. Something alive. This is not a hollow building. Leave it alone.

No bumps but a scratchy tail: rotten, slushy ice. Not hollow.

Currents, the lock and the beacon

ASK THE OPERATOR FOR: how old the fix is · how old the current reading is · where the buoy is compared to the target

When the operator locks onto an iceberg, the chart shows a white dashed circle marked PREDICTED. That is where the machine thinks the ice has drifted since the fix.

The prediction uses the Currents panel and the drift switch:

  • Currents are measured at the buoy. The Currents system must be switched on to take a reading, and it needs a buoy in the water.
  • Currents swirl. A reading taken far from your target is wrong for your target. Put the buoy close to the ice you care about.
  • Choose DEEP for large ice and SURFACE for small ice (page 2).
  • With no reading at all, the prediction does not move.

The scanner needle and the radio signal both get weaker as the prediction drifts away from the real ice. If they fade, take a fresh fix: ping again and lock again.

Firing a beacon

The beacon flies from the observatory and lands where the prediction says the ice will be. It takes a while to arrive (about 80 miles per second).

For a hit you need: a fresh lock (ping, lock, fire quickly), a recent current reading taken near the target, and the right drift setting. A miss falls into the sea.

The radio

ASK THE OPERATOR FOR: the frequency on the display · the three lamp colours, in order
  1. Lock onto a target. The radio listens to the locked target only.
  2. Turn the big dial slowly. Watch the SIGNAL bar. If it never moves anywhere on the dial, this iceberg is silent.
  3. When the bar is nearly full, three lamps flash in order: RED, WHITE, BLUE.

Calibrating the metal scanner

ASK THE OPERATOR FOR: the wind direction on the Currents panel (FROM ...) · a description of each of the four runes

The scanner will not find metal until it is calibrated. The operator must press the four runes on the scanner in the right order. A wrong press shifts the runes and locks the plate for a few seconds.

Press the lightest rune first (lowest weight).

Rune catalogue

Once calibrated, keep the scanner on a locked target. When the bar fills, the lamp shows WORKED METAL FOUND or NO METAL.

Cameras, burrowers and repairs

ASK THE OPERATOR FOR: which camera buttons say GROUND SHAKING · the heat bars

Cameras are fixed: each one always looks the same way. Ice drifts through their view. Click an iceberg in the view to lock onto it.

Burrowers (remorhazes)

  • A camera heats up while you watch it. Its heat bar is on its button.
  • A hot camera draws a remorhaz across the ice. The button says GROUND SHAKING. Switch to that camera and you will see it coming.
  • Switch to another camera and let it cool. A cold camera is not worth the trip, and the beast burrows away.
  • If it arrives, the camera is destroyed.
